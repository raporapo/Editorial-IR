# shorts

Under a minute, for someone who arrived with no context and will leave in two
seconds if nothing happens.

## What it changes

- **It is the only skill that reorders.** `ordering: hook_first` lifts one
  high-intensity moment to the front; everything after it stays in the order it
  happened. Shuffling further would make the piece incoherent, and this format
  is the only one where the first two seconds are worth the cost.
- **Anything that needs context is dropped.** A clip that only makes sense after
  another clip cannot be used, because the viewer did not see the other clip.
- **Redundancy is weighted at -1.0.** In forty seconds there is no room to show
  anything twice.
- **Clips are short and capped at fourteen.** More than that is a slideshow.
- **No establishing shots.** There is no time to establish anything.
