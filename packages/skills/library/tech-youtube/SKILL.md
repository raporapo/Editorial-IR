# tech-youtube

An explanation with something on screen: a terminal, an editor, a device.

Extends `talking-head`, and adds one idea: **the demonstration is the part a
written article cannot replace**, so anything with text on screen is weighted up,
and screen recordings without narration become picture under the voice rather
than clips of their own.

It also front-loads. A viewer decides in the first few seconds, so an opening
that states the point is preferred and preamble is dropped outright.
