# travel-vlog

For a trip: one day or several, told in the order it happened.

## What it changes

- **Feeling is weighted up.** A trip film is not an information film; the
  moments people re-watch are the ones that felt like something.
- **Arriving somewhere new earns an establishing shot.** A viewer who does not
  know where they are cannot follow what happens there.
- **Travel is kept short.** Getting there is context, and three seconds of a
  train window says everything twenty seconds does.
- **A payoff is never cut into.** If a moment pays off something earlier, it
  keeps its whole duration and the reaction that follows it.
- **The ending gets a quarter of the running time.** Trips end somewhere, and a
  film that stops rather than ends feels unfinished however good the middle was.

## Order

Chronological, always. Rearranging someone's day into a more dramatic shape is
the fastest way to lose their trust in the whole tool.
