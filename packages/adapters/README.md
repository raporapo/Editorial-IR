# @editorial-ir/adapters

One EditPlan, three editing applications.

OpenTimelineIO, Premiere Pro (through Final Cut Pro 7 XML) and AviUtl2.

An adapter translates a plan and nothing else — it never plans and never judges. That is what makes a second editor an adapter rather than a rewrite, and why the agent is never allowed to speak to an NLE directly.

Capability differences are negotiated and reported. A dissolve that quietly became a cut is a change to someone's edit, and they are entitled to the list.
