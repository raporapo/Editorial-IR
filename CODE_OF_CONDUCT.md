# Code of conduct

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to make the project better.

Disagreement about technical direction is welcome and expected; this repository
is full of decisions that could reasonably have gone the other way. Argue about
the decision, with reasons.

Not welcome: personal attacks, harassment, or making someone feel unwelcome for
who they are rather than for what they argued.

Maintainers may edit, delete or reject contributions and comments that do not
follow this, and may block accounts that repeatedly do not.

To report something, use the same private channel as
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or contact a maintainer directly.
